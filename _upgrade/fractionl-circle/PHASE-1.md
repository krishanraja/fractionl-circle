# Circle by Fractionl, Phase 1: Locked product + 5X vision + commerce (no code changes)

Date: 2026-05-30. No code changes. Built on the verified PHASE-0 recon plus an 8-agent first-principles 5X pass (7 surface visionaries + 1 commerce-contract spec). This is the unbounded vision. Scope is chosen at the gate, not here.

## 1. Locked product
- ICP (primary): the fractional executive (CMO/CFO/CTO/COO/CHRO), $150K to $1.5M across 2 to 7 concurrent retainers, whose relationships and sellable ideas live in their head.
- Secondary: independent strategy advisors, thought-leader operators (author/keynote/workshop), year-1 fractionals. Anti-ICP: in-house SDR sales teams.
- The one painful problem: warm revenue is lost because there is no system that survives the next client call. The follow-up that would have closed never happens.
- One-sentence pitch: "Talk to it once. It turns what you said into Ideas, cross-references your Ideas against everyone you know overnight, and drops a hand-drafted Move on the right person while you sleep."
- The single magic moment: talk ~90 seconds, get sellable Ideas, and wake to a drafted Move on the right person.
- Core outcomes: stop losing warm leads, sell more by sending less, get your Sunday back, trust your pipeline, reactivate a dormant network, look organized when it matters.
- Tiers: Freemium $0 / Operator $30 / Chief of Staff $79. Stripe account: fractionl_ai.

## 2. The 5X thesis (the spine that unifies every surface)
Two ideas tie the whole vision together. Hold both.

A. ONE LOAD-BEARING FIX: make "overnight" literally true. Today the magic moment is a lie at exactly its seam: FirstVoice seeds Ideas but never people, so a fresh user lands on a `canRun=false` dead-end, and "matches overnight / drafts the Move while you sleep" is actually a manual "Surface Matches" button with no cron and no push. Everything magic depends on closing this. The fix has four parts: (1) mine PEOPLE (and warm edges) from the same 90-second voice note, not just Ideas, so there is always something to match against on night one; (2) run the match + draft the Move server-side automatically (per-user nightly cron + warm-on-unlock incremental run), so the button disappears; (3) wire Web Push (the PWA has none) so the morning "while you slept, here is the one to send" arrives as a notification; (4) stream every model output token by token so nothing is a spinner-then-hard-cut.

B. ONE OWNABLE IDENTITY: the night-shift chief of staff and the sealed letter. Retire the generic-AI skin (single #7C3AED purple, gradients, glow, shimmer, centered heroes, flat glass cards). The metaphor that fits relationship-to-revenue exactly: a thick cream letter on a dark walnut desk, written in real ink overnight, closed with one wax-red seal that appears only when a Move is ready to send. Two materials (Ink Walnut desk, Correspondence cream paper), three ink weights (not three hues), one rationed accent (Seal Red `8 64% 44%`). Source Serif 4 becomes the voice of the chief of staff (everything written FOR you); Satoshi becomes the operator's hand (structure, labels, ledger numerals). Motion has three physical verbs: ink drying (token-streaming reveal replaces every spinner), the seal (the voice orb is a brass signet with mass; it presses down in Seal Red when a Move is ready, lifts and the letter flies off-screen when you send), and folding (cards fold along a crease, no slide/fade). Relationship warmth is paper patina (fresh cream to yellowed to aged-and-creased), not a traffic light. Zero emoji in product surfaces; the seal is the one sanctioned mark. The token system is already HSL-variable-driven, so this is a one-file repaint, not a rewrite.

## 3. The 5X pass, ranked by leverage
Each surface, the essential move, what it kills, and why it feels like magic. Full per-surface visions are in the workflow output.

1. MAGIC MOMENT (highest leverage). Live transcription during the 90 seconds; people-chips and Idea-cards crystallize token by token as you speak; the instant you stop, entities are saved and the cross-reference starts server-side; onboarding ends on a "working while you sleep" night screen (the desk dims, the letter seals), not an empty Today. Next morning a push: "While you slept: Dana at [fintech] is the one. I drafted the message." Tap opens a single full-screen Move, drafted in your voice, streaming in. Two actions: Send, or Make it warmer. Kills: the Ideas-only seed, the dead-end, the manual button, the lying header, the spinner. Magic delta: it knew before you asked, it sounds like you, you converted a voice note into warm outbound before coffee.

2. MATCH ENGINE + MOVE (the daily revenue core). The unit of work becomes "a reason to reach out, right now" = Idea x Person x TRIGGER (a job change, a raise, a new title, a thing you mumbled into a voice note three weeks ago). No trigger, no Move. Matching is continuous and ambient (event-driven + nightly), never a button. The Move is drafted against a VOICE FINGERPRINT (your sent mail/DMs, your FirstVoice recordings, your prior edit-distance corrections) so the test is "could you send it without reading it." The 0-1 score is replaced by a one-line human case plus tappable receipts (the trigger source, the last time you talked, the warm path). One tap creates a real Gmail/Outlook draft or injects into the LinkedIn composer via the existing extension. The loop closes on reply outcomes, not clicks. Restraint is the luxury signal: one great Move or none, never three weak ones. Kills: the visible float, clipboard-and-pray, cold generic drafts, manufactured matches.

3. SUNDAY LETTER -> THE BRIEF (weekly heartbeat + the flywheel's best asset). Pre-render the AUDIO Saturday night for every paying user (free hears a 20-second teaser), prefetched so the Sunday push plays in under 400ms. The push copy is the Brief's own first line, not "your letter is ready." A full-screen narrated player: a specific voice (continuity, opinions, references three weeks ago) narrates 1 to 3 sendable Moves while weighted cards lay themselves on the table in time with the words; you can say "send it" or "not him, why." Then it hands you, unasked, a sanitized, named, public "Signal" post built from your real week (one-tap share to LinkedIn, prerendered page, auto-OG) that makes you look like the most consistent thinker in your category. Private Brief feeds your revenue; public Signal feeds Circle's growth. Kills: audio-as-$79-perk, the buried card-3, the generic 280-word template, the "Sunday Letter" name.

4. CIRCLE, THE PEOPLE GRAPH. Invert it: the user stops feeding the graph; the graph feeds itself from signals the user already generates (calendar attendees, email-header signatures, the call just had, the open LinkedIn tab) and only ever asks "confirm or correct," never "enter." Warmth is a physical temperature that decays on a real curve and cools to the touch; the list sorts by "who is cooling that should not be," not alphabetically. A job change is a five-alarm fire (new budget, new mandate, you are already trusted) that pre-drafts a Move by morning. Ideas overlay the graph so the network renders as ranked expected revenue: "there is $400K of warm pipeline here and you cannot see it." Kills: the Add-Contact form, import-as-chore, static profiles, the day-one empty graph.

5. TODAY / HOME. Invert from "list of matches" to "the one thing now": a single Source Serif sentence in calm negative space ("Talk to Devin Rao today. His Series B just closed and your post-raise growth pod is the exact thing he will need. I drafted the note.") with the Move drafted beneath it, send/tweak/snooze inline. Reasoning streams in under it so you trust it in two seconds. The rest of the day is a quiet receding stack below the fold. The empty new-user state is the strongest pitch in the app ("I have your 6 Ideas, now point me at one place and I will do the rest"), never a dead-end; matches land incrementally as contacts import, with a physical settle. Snooze is intelligent (after his raise closes, when she is back from PTO). Kills: the manual button, the flat equal-weight stack, the terminal empty state, return-null loading.

6. PUBLIC SURFACE + FLYWHEEL. The hero is a live microphone, not a headline: a logged-out visitor holds it, talks for 25 seconds, and watches the REAL engine (transcribe + extract-ideas, anonymous, rate-limited) stream their own words into sellable Idea cards. That gasp is the conversion event. The CTA is a withholding, not a wall: "now the part you cannot see, on your actual network, overnight; want to watch?" The Ideas they just made migrate into the new account. Pulse (the free citable Fractional Working Index) is the funnel mouth and the moat: Circle's anonymized aggregate signal makes Pulse the freshest index in the category, Pulse citations drive traffic to the live mic. Every shared Move and Sunday Letter is an edge-rendered artifact with dynamic OG that recruits the next user. Kills: the centered-hero-three-cards page, the "describe the magic" copy, the stale CRM meta, Pulse-as-afterthought, the fleet pitching from memory.

7. VISUAL IDENTITY + MOTION. Implements the spine (Section 2B) at the pixel level: re-author `src/index.css` to the two-material, three-ink, one-Seal-Red system; delete every generic-AI utility (text-gradient, shimmer, glow, ambient-mesh, glass, the glowing nav button); build a shared `<InkText streaming>` primitive that routes every LLM output through the ink-drying reveal; rebuild the voice orb as the brass signet that doubles as the send-seal; promote serif to the system's voice and tabularize money; design the four states as letter states (loading = ink drying, empty = a blank addressed sheet, error = a returned letter with a pencil note, success = the seal). Commission a nib-weight icon set and a real wordmark (the C closed by an inked ring). Respects reduced-motion (ink lands instant, seal pre-pressed).

## 4. Outbound fleet-commerce contract (5b to 5f, buildable spec)
Full spec in the workflow output; essentials:
- 5b PRODUCT TRUTH: SSG build emits `circle.fractionl.ai/llms.txt` + `/agent.json` from `src/lib/tiers.ts` + a DOCS.md slice (so they cannot drift), with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. agent.json carries product, pitch, ICP, the three tiers with live Stripe price ids, current offer, magic moment, URLs, `stripe_account: fractionl_ai`. The fleet queries it; it never pitches from memory.
- 5c CAPTURE -> STRIPE: an `AttributionContext` (utm_source/medium/campaign/content/term, campaign_id, agent, referrer, landing_path, anonymous_id) captured by an inline pre-hydration script on the SSG surface, first-touch-wins in localStorage + a `circle_aid` cookie that survives the OAuth round-trip. Flushed once on first authed session to a write-once `attribution jsonb` column on the per-user row. Checkout EXTENDS the existing `metadata[supabase_user_id]` stamp with all attribution fields on both customer and subscription.
- 5d READ-BACK: the canonical `attribution.events` row (app=circle, event in landed|signed_up|activated|purchased|refunded|churned, identity + utm + stripe + amount + dedupe_key). landed/signed_up/activated fire server-side (SSG edge layer / Circle helper, never the browser); purchased/refunded/churned fire from the signature-verified webhook off `sub.metadata`. All POST to the OS `ingest-attribution` with `x-attribution-secret`. Deterministic `dedupe_key` per logical event; OS does INSERT ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING. Circle stays emit-only.
- WEBHOOK GAPS TO CLOSE (Phase 3): add Stripe `event.id` dedup (`processed_stripe_events` table), add `invoice.payment_succeeded` (-> purchased) and `charge.refunded` (-> refunded) handlers, emit churned on `subscription.deleted`, wrap emits in try/catch so an ingest failure never 500s Stripe.
- 5e CONTENT FEEDS: split the Sunday Letter into private body + opt-in publishable preview (`public_slug`, `is_publishable` default false, sanitized `preview_text`). Public preview pages at `/letters/<slug>` (edge-rendered, per-page OG, unguessable slug), a JSON Feed at `/feed/sunday-letter.json` and an RSS mirror, reading only publishable previews, never the private narrative or audio.
- 5f LEAD SIGNALS: a consented (opt-in `reengage_consent`, default off) daily signal for Freemium users who activated then stalled (Ideas extracted, no Move sent, idle 7+ days), emitted as a `stalled_free` event to the OS for the fleet to re-engage, with central suppression and one-per-week dedupe.

## 5. Dependencies, risks, sequencing
- Net-new infra the vision needs: per-user nightly cron + event-driven matching; Web Push + a real PWA cache (none today); true SSE token streaming from the edge functions (today they buffer then hard-cut); the Vite SSG public surface + edge OG (locked 5a).
- Highest-sensitivity items (real trust/legal weight): the VOICE FINGERPRINT needs mail/DM read scope (store only a derived style profile + diffs, never raw mail); REAL SENDING (auto-send vs draft-create) should default to draft-create except on Chief of Staff with a generous undo; the PUBLIC SIGNAL must never auto-publish (hard scrub + explicit one-tap approval, off by default); the ANONYMOUS LIVE-MIC demo needs hard rate limiting + duration caps + abuse guards (real Whisper/LLM cost per press); the Circle-feeds-Pulse loop needs genuine k-anonymity + disclosure (legal review).
- Honesty gate: do not ship the "while you sleep" marketing language on the public surface until the overnight cron + push are real, or the first authed experience breaks the promise the hero just made. The live-mic demo and share artifacts can ship before that.
- External-data fragility: LinkedIn change detection is ToS-sensitive and fragile; lean on owned signals first (your own voice logs, calendar, email signatures, opt-in enrichment), treat news/funding as best-effort.
- Trigger data is the make-or-break for Surface 2; internal triggers (voice logs, Sunday Letters) are fully owned and the safest, richest seam to ship first.
