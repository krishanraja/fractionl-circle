# Circle by Fractionl — The Dream-State Vision
### Working name: **Circle — The Path Room**
*Lead-visionary synthesis. 2026-06-17. Grounds: founder interview (locked), the two research corpora, `icp-archetype.md`, `PRODUCT_DIAGNOSIS_2026-06-16.md`. This is the artifact that anchors the build — every line is load-bearing.*

> **The reframe in one breath.** Today's Circle is *network → match → draft the DM → send* — the commodity. The dream-state Circle is *read me → place me → show me the next decision, before I stumble into it → tell me who in my world it touches, and why.* The uniqueness is the **thinking**, never the **doing**. We ship the decision, not the queue.

## 1. North star

**Circle is the chief of staff that senses where you are in the build the moment you arrive, places you on the proven path of fractionals who've already gone where you're headed, and hands you the one decision that closes the gap — armed with the data, the benchmark, the peer evidence, and the few people in your world who matter for it.**

The feeling is **recognition, not motivation — relief, not stimulation.** You open Circle and within ninety seconds the sense lands that *someone competent already studied your whole situation for an hour and is handing you the one true next thing.* Not fourteen cards waiting for you. Not a pep talk. Not an inbox of homework owed. One quiet, fully-reasoned page that says, in effect: *"You're at a known, named place on a route others have walked. Here's where you stand, here's what the people one step ahead of you did, and here's the decision lit in front of you right now."* Premium is restraint. The surface is instrument-grade and almost empty; the engine underneath considered dozens of forks and chose this one for you. Clarity and quiet command — the exact opposite of the unsorted CRM the buyer left the C-suite to escape.

## 2. The core loop

Circle runs on one spine and one verb. The spine is **The Path** — a single, continuous route from where you are to where your comparables got. The verb is **decide** — and deciding advances your marker, re-draws the gap, and re-ranks the people around you. Nothing is "done"; nothing is "owed." You make a call, and the instrument quietly reshapes around the call you made.

The loop, every session:

1. **Be read.** Circle reads your state (segment, goal-clarity, data-richness, what-matters-most) off your real data and words. You configure nothing.
2. **Be placed.** Your marker lands on the Path among the faint tracks of structurally-identical comparables — same function, tenure, revenue band, niche. The fork you're standing on is lit; the forks ahead are dim previews ("you'll face this next").
3. **See the gap.** A single instrument card names the precise delta between you and the people who already crossed your next fork — *"they fixed-scoped before scaling; you bill hourly," "they had four warm relationships in fintech; you have one," "they charged $14K; you charge $9K."* Every gap line is a doorway into the decision that closes it.
4. **Decide one thing.** Tap the lit fork. It opens into a full decision surface: the fork in one sentence, your numbers against the benchmark, what comparable fractionals actually did at this exact position (and what stood in their way), a recommendation with 2–3 branches, and the handful of people the decision touches. You commit to a choice — never a task, never a draft.
5. **Watch it advance.** Your marker moves. The gap re-draws. The living inner circle re-ranks around your new direction. The next fork lights, with its landmine pre-surfaced ("the month-6 crisis is ahead; survivors had a second warm conversation in motion by month four — you're at month four").

**The spine IS the grow-with-you journey.** A New fractional's *Launch Path* (corporate-exit → niche → first offer → first client → survive month six → reach three clients) terminates exactly where the Seasoned *Leverage Path* begins (three stable clients → productize → pod → equity → owned audience). As the user actually grows, their marker walks off the end of one map and onto the next — no re-onboarding, no product switch. The slow, per-person payoff of watching your own marker advance along a route you can *see* is the retention engine: the value compounds per-person and is non-portable. Leave, and you abandon the only place that holds the reasoning behind every call you've made.

> **Anti-queue law (the deepest founder wound).** There is **exactly one lit fork at a time.** When a new decision becomes live, the previous unresolved one is silently absorbed/archived into the decision ledger — never a visible backlog, never a count, never two things glowing at once. The Path is a navigation instrument, not a progress bar and not an inbox.

## 3. The adaptive engine — "The Read"

Circle holds a live, **visible and editable** internal model of the user it calls **The Read** — a continuously-updated state vector across four axes. Making the user-model an explicit, correctable panel ("Here's what I've understood about where you are") is what turns morphing-UI from creepy manipulation into earned trust: the user trusts the reshaping because they can see and correct what drives it, and every correction trains The Read permanently. Each axis drives *concrete reshaping*, not cosmetic copy.

### Axis 1 — SEGMENT (a chasm, switched not dialed)
The research is unambiguous: *"the gap between these two ICPs is not a spectrum — it is a chasm. Products that conflate them fail both."* So segment **flips the entire instrument.**
- **Signal:** inferred from tenure + client count + retainer/revenue band + niche-named + the *verbs in their opening words* ("just left," "first client," "runway" → New; "third client," "plateau," "productize," "equity" → Seasoned). Critically, contact-count is **never** the segmenter — the corpus notes new entrants often arrive with fat corporate address books and seasoned operators between anchor clients can look thin.
- **Reshape:** New gets the **Launch Path** organized around *survival gravity* — who unlocks the first/second client, who is living proof the model works, the month-6 cliff visible ahead. Seasoned gets the **Leverage Path** organized around *leverage gravity* — productize, pod, equity, concentration-risk, beating the three-client ceiling. **Same engine, inverted physics.** A Seasoned exec is **never** throttled through New-grade scaffolding: the instant the system reads three clients + 24 months, it boots straight into plateau-break comparables **at full power, session one.** A misread is **one tap to correct**, and the correction is permanent — this is the escape hatch that protects a $45K/mo operator from ever being handed a "commit to a lane before month six" insult.

### Axis 2 — GOAL-CLARITY (clear / none / browsing)
- **Clear goal** → you're dropped onto the exact segment of the Path that goal lives on, and Circle pressure-tests the goal against benchmarks.
- **No goal** → Circle leads with the highest-leverage decision your coordinates expose and names *why this, why now* ("most people standing exactly here are deciding X next — and here's the one most get wrong").
- **Just browsing** → the **gallery view**: anonymized real routes of comparables, browsable, ending on one question — *"which of these is the life you're building toward?"* This is the softest surface and the one most at risk of rotting into a content feed, so it is governed by a hard rule: **browsing always terminates in a single chooseable direction, never an infinite scroll.** The moment something lands, it converts into a placed marker.

### Axis 3 — DATA-RICHNESS (sets resolution, never gates access)
- **Thin / just-starting** → the Path is drawn from population benchmarks and peer-path archetypes; you're positioned by the few facts known (function, ex-title, tenure). Circle is **honest about what it's inferring** and asks for the *one* input that unlocks the most. The whoa still lands on zero imported data, because benchmark placement alone is enough to name the next fork — this is the cold-start cure for the New fractional whose own network is too thin to carry the inner-circle engine in session one.
- **Rich (imported network + win history)** → the Path sharpens to a tight comparable cohort ("fractional CMOs, B2B SaaS, ex-VP, ~$15K retainers"), and the gap analysis gets specific down to *which relationships* and *which offer* — leaning on **ideas × network synthesis** to surface non-obvious people you'd never have ranked yourself.
- **The engine always degrades gracefully: blank-but-honest beats confident-but-fabricated.** (See §8.)

### Axis 4 — WHAT-MATTERS-MOST (which decision rises)
A single learned priority decides which fork is promoted to *lit* and what the first-session payoff is, scored by **(felt-urgency × reversibility-cost × readiness-of-evidence)** with a **confidence floor**. A pricing-anxious user sees the pricing fork lit first with the imposter-killing benchmark; a plateau-anxious Seasoned sees the leverage move first; an isolation-anxious user sees the relationship fork first. The rest are held silently in the ledger — never stacked on screen.

**Net:** The Read reshapes the **home** (which fork is lit, which Path you're on), the **first session** (the one composed payoff), the **decisions surfaced** (segment- and trigger-aware forks), and the **power level** (full seasoned grade on day one, never patronized, never throttled).

## 4. The decision-space — all four, end-to-end, proactively surfaced

Every decision domain lives as a **fork-type** on the Path, opening into a Decision surface armed with the same loop: *name the decision before the wall → plot your data against the benchmark → show the peer path and the named obstacle → recommend → resolve in place → re-rank the people it touches.* All four are co-equal and first-class — the Path does not privilege the scaling story; "what-matters-most" relights whichever fork serves the user's actual direction (a Seasoned exec who wants to **re-position**, not scale, gets the niche fork lit, not railroaded onto "nine of eleven productized").

### (a) Offer & pricing — *kill pricing imposter syndrome with distributional fact*
> *"Your profile's median retainer is $14K. You're at $9K. The comparables who broke your next fork were at $15K — and the ones who quoted under $8K spent the next year unable to raise it. Here's the fixed-scope package nine of them sold, and the order they built it."*

Anchored in corpus benchmarks: seasoned retainers **$10–18K/mo, avg $12–15K at year three**; New fractionals plan **$50–120K year one, $150–250K year two**; **fixed-scope offers close ~3× faster than hourly** (LinkedIn practitioner signal — tiered as *expert opinion*, not dressed as hard telemetry; see §8). Productization is surfaced *the moment* the data shows bespoke-everything drag. The decision is the package + price, committed.

### (b) ICP, niche & positioning — *the keystone New fork; the obvious-choice engine*
> *"You said you 'help companies grow.' That describes ~100,000 people on LinkedIn and resonates with none. Based on the three companies you've actually worked with and where your network concentrates, the lane where you'd be the obvious choice is B2B-SaaS-at-Series-B-with-broken-demand-gen."*

Grounded in the corpus law that **niche specificity is the only durable differentiator** and that referrals literally **cannot fire** without a narrow, referable lane (92.8% of clients come via referral; 19.2% cold). The cost of paralysis is made urgent and concrete (the corpus's 3–6-month revenue gap). Uses peer paths of who niched into which wedge and what it did to their pipeline. **Your network already contains your niche** — when seven of eleven warmest contacts cluster in one vertical, that's a positioning decision hiding in plain sight, resolved with data the user already has and zero outreach.

### (c) Network strategy — *who to keep close, framed as a decision*
Feeds and is fed by the living inner circle (§5): *"To walk this fork you need a warm path into Series-B SaaS founders. You have one — keep David close; he's two intros from three of them."* Shaped by the channel hierarchy the corpus proves (network activation converts earliest; warm referrals are 92.8% of pipeline; cold outreach fails near-universally at 19.2%) and the network-ceiling timing (referral pool saturates **12–24 months** in — *"here's where your next channel comes from before the well runs dry"*). Output is always **who + why**, never an outreach task.

### (d) Scaling & leverage — *the Seasoned / paid-OS tier*
> *"You're at the three-client ceiling — the structural ceiling, not a hustle problem. Of comparable fractional CFOs who passed your next fork, six built a 1099 associate pod and roughly doubled capacity at a margin hit; five fixed-scoped their highest-repeat engagement and held solo at higher margin. Nine of eleven productized **before** touching the pod question. And one thing first: your anchor client is 58% of revenue — de-risk that before you take attention off delivery."*

The full structural decision-space: productize → pod/associate → equity/advisory (409A, vesting) → owned-audience inbound, each with corpus benchmarks (the three-client sweet spot, >50%-single-client concentration as the top failure mode, pod economics, equity complexity) and the divergent branches comparables actually took. Every decision ends in a committed structural call, not a to-do list.

**Proactivity is the chief-of-staff move:** Circle raises the pricing-floor decision *before* the under-charging trap, the niche decision *before* the month-6 stall, the concentration-risk decision *before* the anchor client wobbles. It pulls the future fork into the present, armed with what peers did and the landmine they hit.

## 5. The living inner circle — ideas × network, and how it stays NOT a CRM

The inner circle is the relationship engine **in service of the fork you're standing on.** It answers one question: *given where you're headed right now, who in your world matters most, and why?* It is **ideas × network synthesis** made tangible — your current direction (the decisions you're walking) is intersected with your real network (warmth, recency, shared history, role, company stage, trajectory, mutual density) to surface a **small, ranked, evolving set** of people, each carrying a **role** and a one-line WHY:

- **PROOF** — living evidence the path works (the imposter-syndrome killer: *"Priya sold the exact productized offer at the price you're scared to charge — she is the proof, not the prospect"*).
- **UNLOCK** — opens a door or a pocket of demand (*"sits one intro from three Series-B SaaS founders — the exact buyer your new lane needs"*).
- **MULTIPLIER** — pod partner / cross-referral / equity path (corpus: CFOs and COOs refer CMOs).
- **RISK** — concentration or stagnation (*"this anchor is now 58% of your revenue"*).
- **MIRROR** — a peer one step ahead on your exact path (the corpus's #1 buying signal for both ICPs: peers-as-practitioners).

It **re-ranks the instant your direction changes.** Commit to a fintech-CFO niche and the three generalist contacts quietly recede while the two people already deep in fintech rise, each with a fresh WHY. You watch your inner circle *think alongside your decisions.* For thin-data users it leans on peer-path archetypes to name the *kind* of person to keep close; for deep-data users it surfaces buried non-obvious people. Crucially, the inner circle is **cross-referenced to the comparable cohort** — *"the people who broke this plateau leaned on a relationship like David's"* — so it's peer-validated relationship strategy, not generic prioritization.

### Why it can never become a CRM (the rule the runner-up concepts left unstated)
The output is **who + why + role**, and then it **stops.** No drafted message, no send, no sequence, no pipeline stage, no last-contacted column. But the load-bearing distinction — the one a skeptical founder will probe — is this:

> **The inner circle is deliberately STATELESS about whether you acted.** It never tracks "did you talk to David yet?", never shows a checkbox, never nags "you haven't contacted X." Each session it **re-reads warmth from your real calendar/email signal** and re-ranks from scratch. A CRM persists your *actions on people*; the inner circle persists *nothing about your behavior* — it only re-reads the world. That decoupling is what keeps "keep David close" a strategic read rather than a task with a status. The doing happens in your own life, in your own voice. We tell you the person and the reason; the conversation stays yours.

And the "strategic question to hold" attached to a person is about **your decision**, rendered to **you** — never addressed *to* the person, never copyable as a message. There is no semantic inch between it and a talking-points generator, because it is structurally not a message.

## 6. The surfaces — radically simple on top, exhaustive underneath

Three surfaces. No queue anywhere. The user touches three things; the system reasons over thousands.

### (1) THE PATH — the home and the soul
A single quiet route from where you are to where your comparables got, rendered as a **clean navigational instrument** — *not* a childish progress bar, *not* a swooshing planetarium of twinkling stars (instrument-grade ≠ animated toy; the *idea* of re-ranking on direction-change is right, the literal sci-fi rendering is a trap). Your marker sits on the line. The fork you're on is lit; forks ahead are dim previews. Behind the route, faint, are the anonymized tracks of your comparable cohort — you see where most went and where the minority broke off. One screen answers *where am I, where are they, what's next.*

### (2) THE GAP — the anti-generic-AI primitive
A single instrument card naming the literal state-delta between you and the comparables who crossed your next fork. **This is the structural cure for the "both fractional executives" tautology the diagnosis caught** — the engine can *only* speak in deltas and sequences, so a content-free role-equivalence claim is mathematically impossible to render. Each gap line is a doorway into the decision that closes it.

### (3) THE DECISION ROOM — where a fork resolves
The full-screen working surface: the fork in one sentence, your numbers vs. the benchmark band, the peer-path evidence + the named obstacle, the inner-circle people it touches, a recommendation with branches. You decide here; it **resolves in place** (the diagnosis's worst sin was Ask bouncing you away) — the marker advances, the decision is written to the ledger, the circle re-ranks. The Decision Room contains the "exhaustive underneath": the Reasoning conversation where Circle plays chief of staff, asks the one sharpening question, shows the benchmark distribution, and runs *"if you do X, here's the likely consequence based on peers."*

**Mobile** = the Path as one calm card you read on a Sunday morning; one lit fork, the gap, decide. Bespoke, zero-scroll, one-decision-at-a-time.
**Desktop = the command-centre** (the diagnosis's one genuine "build"): a three-region instrument — the Path as a spine you can scrub, the living inner circle as a persistent side rail, the Decision Room as a focused full panel with the benchmark band as a live dial and the decision ledger as a quiet history. Dark-first, premium, GoBold visual layer. Never a stretched single-column mobile mirror.

Everywhere else — capture, network import, settings — is ambient and folds away.

## 8. What Circle is NOT — anti-commodity boundary, stated as product law

These are not preferences. They are **laws**; violating any one is a failed build.

1. **NOT a CRM.** No pipeline stages, no deal columns, no last-contacted field, no contact roster to maintain. The inner circle is **who + why + role**, stateless about your behavior, re-read from real signal each session. *(This is the law the strongest concept left unstated and the one most likely to sink the build if violated — see §5.)*
2. **NOT an outreach tool.** Zero message drafting, zero send, zero sequences, zero "Email Sarah," zero mailto. Circle stops at the decision and the relationship read; the doing stays with the human, in their own voice. *(The diagnosis flagged the empty-body mailto and "See draft" as exactly the commodity trap to abandon.)*
3. **NOT a queue / inbox / backlog.** No "14 Matches waiting for you," no `matches.map()` wall, no "Find more matches," no count in any H1, no "Also today" ghost tail. **Exactly one lit fork**; the previous one is silently absorbed.
4. **NOT an emotional-support coach.** It kills imposter syndrome with **distributional fact** — the benchmark band, the peer evidence, your own numbers — not affirmations or feelings-talk. Recognition and orientation, never reassurance.
5. **NOT generic AI.** Every claim is grounded in real state, real network signal, and real peer-path data. The engine speaks in **deltas and sequences**, so "you both are fractional executives" is structurally impossible. **The honesty rule is absolute and mechanized, not asserted:** any claim that can't be grounded is **blanked, not confabulated** — and this extends *especially* to peer-path. We say *"benchmark says"* (cohort pattern, sourced) and never *"eleven people who started exactly where you are"* until the real cohort *n* exists. Stat provenance is **tiered** on the instrument: hard data vs. survey vs. expert-opinion (the ex-CFO buyer must never catch a single LinkedIn-post number dressed as a metric). One visible fabrication poisons trust in every reading.
6. **NOT a throttled funnel.** A Seasoned exec is never walked through beginner scaffolding to "unlock" power; misread is one tap to correct.
7. **NOT childish, NOT a gamified XP bar, NOT a stretched-mobile desktop.** Instrument-grade, quiet, premium, one true thing at a time.

> The uniqueness is the **THINKING** about what to decide and who matters for where you're headed — not the **DOING** of any outreach. If a screen ever feels like the unsorted inbox the buyer left the C-suite to escape, the concept has failed.

## 9. Why it wins

**Vs. Nynch and relationship-led CRMs (~$50–100/mo, the named incumbent built for this exact buyer).** A CRM — even a "relationship-led" one with a Say/Do ratio — has **no concept of where you're headed.** It sorts on recency, warmth, and pipeline stage; it manages contacts you already know you should talk to. Circle's inner circle sorts on **your latest strategic commitment** — re-projecting your network against your *direction* and re-ranking the instant that direction moves. That is something a CRM is structurally incapable of, because it has no decision spine to sort against. Circle is not a better contact manager; it is the layer **above** the contact manager — the thinking the CRM assumes you've already done.

**Vs. generic AI ("this is just ChatGPT with my contacts").** The diagnosis proved the live app's fatal tell: it fed the model only `{name, company, title}` and confabulated "both fractional executives." Circle's architecture makes that tell **impossible**: the Gap card and the inner circle can only speak in literal state-deltas and corpus-sourced cohort patterns. A claim with no grounding is blanked. The borrowed-conviction engine raises the confabulation stakes precisely where it raises the value, so the honesty rule is mechanized at the surface, not left to prompt discipline.

**Vs. the rest of the fractional toolchain (8–15 siloed tools, none AI-native, none covering the strategic upstream).** The corpus names the open opportunity exactly: the lifecycle from *direction → who matters → what to decide* is unserved; existing tools handle the downstream doing (proposals, contracts, invoicing) and nobody handles the upstream **thinking.** Circle owns the highest-value, least-served, hardest-to-copy layer — and refuses the commodity downstream entirely.

**The moat is the data flywheel.** The comparable-cohort dataset — real fractional trajectories keyed by segment × function × niche × fork → what they chose → what happened — is the load-bearing asset. It starts seeded from the two corpora and sharpens with every user's consented, anonymized logged path. The longer Circle runs, the tighter the cohorts, the sharper the peer-path, the deeper each person's WHY — and the value compounds **per-user and is non-portable.** That is the retention spine and the defensibility in one object.

## 10. Data gaps to plug (we plug as we go; assume all obtainable)

1. **The comparable-cohort dataset — the moat, and the one genuine research lift.** Real fractional trajectories tagged by function, tenure, revenue band, niche, trigger-type (Pushed/Pulled/Lifestyle/Encore), and the *sequence* of decisions and moves (plateau-break ordering, time-to-second-client, productize-then-pod). **Bootstrap from corpus benchmarks day one** (three-client plateau, $14K median, month-6 crisis, channel hierarchy, 3×-on-fixed-scope), then grow the flywheel from consented user paths. **Until *n* is real, present as "benchmark says," never "eleven people like you."**
2. **Network graph + signal — wiring, not acquisition.** The diagnosis confirms `last_interaction_at`, `warmth`, `response_rate`, and the `signals` table already load but never reach the model (`buildPrompt` sends only name/company/title). **Wire these through first** — it is the single highest-ROI fix and the precondition for the living re-rank. Then enrich via connected LinkedIn/calendar/email *metadata only* (recency, warmth, shared-company, source, trajectory, mutual density) — read-only, **never** to draft or send.
3. **Person enrichment** — role, company stage, trajectory, mutual-connection density (Apollo / PDL / LinkedIn-class) to compute leverage and assign the five roles (PROOF/UNLOCK/MULTIPLIER/RISK/MIRROR).
4. **Profile-to-cohort matching + segment classifier** — firmographic enrichment + opening-capture NLP to place a thin-data user accurately, plus the New-vs-Seasoned classifier (verbs + firmographics + revenue/clients) with a **confidence floor and the one-tap correction escape hatch**. Blank-when-unsure beats misclassify.
5. **Pricing / benchmark tables, refreshable** — retainer bands, package close-rates, year-1/year-3 income curves, plateau thresholds, by function and niche — kept current via a Pulse/FWI-style feed so the instrument never goes stale.
6. **Landmine / failure-mode library** — the named risks at each fork (month-6 crisis, scope creep, generalist trap, scale-hours-first regret, >50% concentration) mined from the community-signal corpus, for proactive pre-surfacing.
7. **The decision ledger — net-new, central.** A durable per-user record of every committed decision *and its reasoning*, so briefs compound and never repeat ("you committed to the SaaS lane Tuesday; here's the first consequence"). This is simultaneously the grow-with-you spine and the non-portable retention asset. **Crucially, it stores decisions and reasoning — never behavioral state about whether you contacted a person** (that would rebuild the CRM). And the win/milestone signal that drives the Launch→Leverage shift must be captured as *goal/state input only* — never wired as both amount and target like today's broken Streams loop.

## 11. Open forks for the founder (lock the rule, then it builds itself)
