# Circle by Fractionl: agent briefing

Verified against the active product: 2026-08-11.

This is the fast-start briefing for an autonomous agent. Read [docs/PRODUCT.md](docs/PRODUCT.md) for product truth and [docs/MARKETING_AND_SALES.md](docs/MARKETING_AND_SALES.md) before creating marketing or sales material. Release claims require evidence from [docs/DELIVERY_STATE.md](docs/DELIVERY_STATE.md).

## Promise

> Remember anyone. Find the right person when they can help.

Circle helps independent operators save people from whatever they have, remember why each person mattered, browse everyone, and bring useful people back while searching or working through a business idea.

## Working audience

The primary audience is a commercial hypothesis, not proven market evidence: fractional executives and independent operators who meet useful people but do not want to maintain a CRM.

Do not target sales teams that need deal stages, seat management, pipeline reporting, or automated outreach.

## Live product

- One signed-in workspace with People, Ideas, and You always one tap away
- Complete saved-person browsing and search
- Add by text, link, email, phone inside a note, voice, photo, supported device contact, or Android PWA share
- LinkedIn or CRM file import plus Google and Microsoft connection entry points
- Optional `Where did you meet?` context
- Exact saved-name recall without an LLM or paid search dependency
- Broader provider-backed matching with grounded local recovery
- Saved ideas, new idea capture, exact-run reopen, and relevant-person results
- Match reasons based on saved evidence
- Contact actions only when a saved channel exists and only when the user chooses
- Profile, connected contacts, reminders, appearance, privacy, and account settings
- Shared voice input on useful free-form fields
- Public privacy and terms routes plus signed-in consent, export, and deletion controls
- Free and Pro in the code catalogue; Pro is listed at $39 per month in `src/lib/tiers.ts`

## Important limits

- Native iOS share-sheet intake and an official Apple Shortcut are not shipped.
- Enrichment, broader search, and idea ranking may depend on external providers.
- Sparse saved details can produce a limited local result.
- Circle does not guarantee interest, availability, relationship strength, complete contact data, or an introduction.
- Circle never sends a message automatically.
- No current evidence supports customer counts, conversion, time saved, revenue impact, or success-rate claims.
- Do not advertise a trial, a $30 plan, a $79 plan, or automatic market monitoring as a proven outcome.

## Positioning

Circle is personal contact memory for independent operators. It asks for less upkeep than a CRM, preserves the user's own context, and retrieves saved people from evidence rather than treating a fluent web guess as relationship truth.

Lead with the outcome, then ease, context, use, and trust. Do not lead with AI, dedupe, enrichment, providers, schemas, or internal idea-engine names.

## Safe copy

### One sentence

Circle helps independent operators save people from whatever they have and find the right saved person when they can help.

### Short description

Add a name, note, link, voice memo, or photo. Circle keeps the details together, lets you browse everyone, and brings useful people back when you search or work through an idea. You see why someone matched, and you decide what happens next.

### Call to action

Start with one person you do not want to forget.

## Agent rules

- Treat product capabilities as facts only when [PRODUCT.md](docs/PRODUCT.md) and current source support them.
- Treat the audience, pain, and messaging as hypotheses until customer evidence proves them.
- Use `can` only for a live, verified path. Use `may` for enrichment or provider-backed outcomes.
- Never invent a result, statistic, customer, testimonial, integration, urgency, or guarantee.
- Never imply that a saved person is interested, available, close to the user, or willing to make an introduction.
- Never describe roadmap work or historical positioning as current.
- Draft only unless separate approval allows publishing, sending, spending, or changing an external system.
- Link only live routes: `/`, `/auth`, `/share-contact`, `/privacy`, and `/terms`.
